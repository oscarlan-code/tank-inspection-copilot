# Scratch Previews

Scratch previews are temporary, unProduct-approved experiments.

Rules:
- keep them out of stable `V1 beta` packages
- keep them explicitly named `Scratch`
- do not treat them as approved product direction
- replace or delete them once the real screen direction is agreed

Current product scratch preview:
- `app/src/main/java/ai/laiq/tankinspection/presentation/v3product/scratch/ProductScratchRoofLayoutPreviewScreen.kt`
- `app/src/main/java/ai/laiq/tankinspection/v3product/scratch/ProductScratchPreviewActivity.kt`

Purpose:
- quarantine exploratory UI work
- prevent confusion between approved Product screens and draft experiments
