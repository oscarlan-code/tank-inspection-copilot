The earlier preparation inherited a roof-only measurement fixture. Schema,
copy and value-preservation checks passed without establishing whole-report
input completeness. That omission, rather than lost UI data, caused the empty
shell section. The former input, output and worker responses are retained under
`data/tk5452-inspector-evaluation-v2/revisions/before-shell-measurements/`.

The preparation now requires eight unique course/direction locations and all
five readings at each, with positive finite values and explicit millimetres.
Shared native routing assigns those rows to shell thickness. An evaluation-only
display projection transposes the native rows into two North/East/South/West

to 0.01 mm), and maximum are calculated from these readings and labelled as
calculations, rather than app capture. All 24 summary cells match the reference.

their calculation inputs; this correction does not supply an engineering assessment.

now states that 11 of 21 sections have inputs and ten have none. Partial sections
show their gaps above the draft; missing-input sections show a clear notice
instead of an empty report page. These counts are input availability, not

Further live runs are blocked while known input gaps remain. `--replay` makes
zero AI calls. This correction reused the three prior responses only after exact
comparison of worker inputs excluding package-scoped evidence IDs. Text, entity,
