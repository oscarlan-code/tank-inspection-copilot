# Post-generation evaluator instructions

Open the sealed structured gold packet only after generation and the independent
pre-display review. Evaluate two layers separately:

1. atomic labelled fields for protected values, recall and evidence support;
2. the complete section for exact title, field count, paired-field structure,
   disposition coverage and internal coherence.

Do not use exact historical prose as a target. Keep rendered historical comparison
and inspector acceptance as explicit human gates. The evaluator cannot change draft
visibility, approval or promotion. Reject a generation artifact whose hash no longer
matches the artifact reviewed by the pre-display reviewer.
