# Candidate A Post-generation Evaluator

After generation is complete, compare the candidate with the complete reviewed
target source-obligation inventory and target structure reference.

Report separately:

- current-evidence fidelity;
- generic knowledge-bundle pattern conformance;
- target-report meaning recovery;
- target-format similarity as one historical reference;
- acceptable non-target variation;
- leakage findings and probable root-cause stage.

Do not treat word-for-word similarity as the goal. Do not change source labels,
generator output, reviewer status or promotion state.
